Who I Am

About Me

I'm Daril Quervo, a tech-oriented developer and digital problem solver. I enjoy building useful websites, mobile applications, and automation tools that help people and businesses work smarter.

I'm from Cebu, Philippines, and currently in Japan as a skilled worker under the Philippine government's official Technical Intern Training Program (TITP), building earthquake-resistant homes to Japanese precision standards. Outside work hours I taught myself web and app development and shipped real products to the App Store and Google Play.

Before Japan I spent over a decade in hands-on trades — registered Master Electrician, carpenter and maintenance technician. That background shapes how I work today: on time, careful, and focused on getting the details right.

FAQ

Before You Reach Out

Quick answers to common questions.

Why are you based in Japan?

I'm here through an official Philippine government skilled-worker program, working in Japanese construction. It's taught me punctuality, precision, and follow-through the Japanese way — and my evenings and weekends are dedicated to my clients. Japan's timezone also means I overlap with US evenings, AU business hours, and EU mornings.

How fast do you reply?

Within 1–2 days at most, usually much faster. For active clients, same day.

What makes you different from other VAs?

Most VAs stop where the tech starts. I've published six apps to the App Store and Google Play and run 10+ live websites — so when your site breaks, your tools need connecting, or a workflow needs automating, I handle it instead of telling you to hire a developer.

How does payment work?

Monthly packages are paid at the start of the month. One-off tasks: deposit up front, balance on delivery. I'll always confirm scope and price in writing before starting.

Can we start small?

Yes — start with a single small task or the Starter package. No long-term commitment; earn my keep first.
