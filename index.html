About

Discipline from the job site. Skills from the screen.

I'm Daril — a virtual assistant and self-taught developer from Cebu, Philippines, currently in Japan as a skilled worker under the Philippine government's official Technical Intern Training Program (TITP), building earthquake-resistant homes to Japanese precision standards.

Outside work hours, I taught myself web and app development and shipped real products to the App Store and Google Play. That combination — Japanese work discipline plus self-taught tech skills — is what I bring to every client: I show up on time, follow through, communicate clearly, and solve problems other assistants have to outsource.

Before Japan I spent over a decade in hands-on trades: registered Master Electrician, carpenter, and maintenance technician. I know how small businesses actually run, because I've worked inside them.

FAQ

Before you reach out

Why are you based in Japan?

I'm here through an official Philippine government skilled-worker program, working in Japanese construction. It's taught me punctuality, precision, and follow-through the Japanese way — and my evenings and weekends are dedicated to my clients. Japan's timezone also means I overlap with US evenings, AU business hours, and EU mornings.

How fast do you reply?

Within 1–2 days at most, usually much faster. For active clients, same day.

What makes you different from other VAs?

Most VAs stop where the tech starts. I've published six apps to the App Store and Google Play and run three live websites — so when your site breaks, your tools need connecting, or a workflow needs automating, I handle it instead of telling you to hire a developer.

How does payment work?

Monthly packages are paid at the start of the month. One-off tasks: deposit up front, balance on delivery. I'll always confirm scope and price in writing before starting.

Can we start small?

Yes — start with a single small task or the Starter package. No long-term commitment; earn my keep first.
