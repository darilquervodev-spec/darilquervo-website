About Me

FAQ

Before You Reach Out

Quick answers to common questions.

Why hire you instead of a bigger agency?

You work directly with the person who builds your project, with no account managers in between. I've published six mobile apps (four on Google Play, two on the App Store) and run six live websites and web apps, so I know what it takes to ship, not just prototype. And 15 years in the trades taught me to understand the job first, measure twice, and deliver something that works on day one.

Are you the same person behind DarskeeWorks?

Yes. DarskeeWorks is my own studio, and darilquervo.com is my personal portfolio. Same person, same email, same LINE. Use DarskeeWorks for website packages and the template shop, or contact me here about any project.

How much does a project cost?

Website packages start at ₱4,999 (USD quotes on request). Apps, business software and AI projects vary in scope, so after a short chat you get a written proposal with scope, timeline and a fixed price. Monthly support plans start at $80.

How fast do you reply?

Within 1–2 business days, usually faster. Active clients get same-day replies.

How does payment work?

Projects: a deposit to start and the balance on delivery, as set out in your written proposal. Monthly support plans are paid at the start of each month. I always confirm scope and price in writing before starting.

Why are you based in Japan?

I'm from Cebu, Philippines, and work in Japan's construction industry, currently building earthquake-resistant homes with ICHIJO Homes. My evenings and weekends go to client projects, and Japan's timezone overlaps with Philippine, Australian and Asian business hours, US evenings and EU mornings.

Can we start small?

Yes. Start with a landing page, a small automation or one month of support. There's no long-term commitment, so you can see how I work first.
